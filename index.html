About

A high-impact gathering for the next generation of financial leaders

Candlestick Summit brings together forex traders, crypto and stock investors, fintech enthusiasts, young entrepreneurs, students and builders.

Our vision is to educate, connect and empower the next generation of financial leaders and business owners in Nigeria through practical learning, meaningful conversations, and access to relevant brands and opportunities.

Speakers

Voices shaping finance and technology

Femi Oyewunmi

Trader & Finance Coach

Founder, School of Wealth

Femi Oyewunmi

Oluwafemi (Femi) Oyewunmi is a wealth educator, finance professional and author, and the founder of School of Wealth NG. Through his Money Monday financial-education series he teaches people practical ways to earn, save, invest and build lasting wealth.

Sir Mapy

Founder, SMC DAO & Wikicat Coin

Builder, Peniwallet / Peniremit

Sir Mapy

Sir Mapy is the pseudonymous crypto builder behind SMC DAO (“Sir Mapy & Co.”), a community of “Believers” he founded in December 2021. He created Wiki Cat (WKC) on BNB Chain as a tutorial token to teach the community how tokens are made, and is building Peniwallet and Peniremit.

Babatunde Ajiboye

Asst. Director, CBN · Digital Asset Regulation

Secretary, Nigeria Electronic Fraud Forum

Babatunde Ajiboye

Babatunde Ajiboye is an Assistant Director at the Central Bank of Nigeria, working on digital asset regulation, fintech and the payments ecosystem. He also serves as Secretary of the Nigeria Electronic Fraud Forum (NeFF), which brings regulators, banks and payment providers together to fight electronic fraud.

Philips Akinyele

Co-founder & CTO, Payaza

Co-founder, 78 Group

Philips Akinyele

Philips Akinyele is the Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Payaza, the Nigerian payments company, where he leads the technology behind its payment infrastructure. He is also a Co-founder of 78 Group, and builds at the intersection of technology and business.

Ezra Solomon

Group Chief Compliance Officer, Unified Payments

Former Senior Payments System Risk & Compliance Specialist, CBN

Ezra Solomon

Ezra Solomon (BSc, MBA, FCA, CISA, CRISC, CIA, CAMS, CFE, CAMS-Audit) is a finance, governance, risk and compliance expert with over two decades of experience across consulting, financial services and the Central Bank of Nigeria. He is Group Chief Compliance Officer of Unified Payments, and a trainer and advisor who helps payment-ecosystem organisations build compliance frameworks that meet local and international standards.

Babatunde Oluremi Ogunlaja

Former Deputy Director, Development Finance Department

Central Bank of Nigeria

Babatunde Oluremi Ogunlaja

Babatunde Oluremi Ogunlaja is a former Deputy Director in the Development Finance Department of the Central Bank of Nigeria, the department that drives the bank’s development-finance and financial-inclusion work.
