About

A high-impact gathering for the next generation of financial leaders

Candlestick Summit brings together forex traders, crypto and stock investors, fintech enthusiasts, young entrepreneurs, students and builders.

Our vision is to educate, connect and empower the next generation of financial leaders and business owners in Nigeria through practical learning, meaningful conversations, and access to relevant brands and opportunities.
